# Privacy Policy — Dice Roller

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## Introduction

Dice Roller is a simple dice-rolling application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

## Information We Collect

Dice Roller does not collect, request, or transmit personal information to the developer. The app does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, or email address.

## How the App Works

Dice rolls are generated locally on your device. The app does not send dice results or personal information to a server.

## Data Sharing

The app does not sell, share, or disclose users' personal information to third parties.

## Data Storage and Security

Dice Roller does not maintain a developer-operated database of users' personal information. Dice rolls are processed on your device.

## Children's Privacy

Dice Roller does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updated policy will be published on this page.
